THE PROCESS05 / 07
01 / 05

Every project moves through a structured sequence — no ambiguity, no surprises.

Brief & References

We lock the direction before a single polygon is placed — concept, references, style, constraints and target platform all defined up front.

  • Concept alignment
  • Reference board
  • Technical targets

Blockout & Form

Silhouette and proportion are resolved early. The form must read correctly from every angle before detail work begins.

  • Silhouette pass
  • Proportion lock
  • Scale validation

Sculpt & Detail

High-frequency detail, surface breakup and material intent — the stage where an asset earns its character.

  • High-poly sculpt
  • Surface detail
  • Material direction

Retopo & Optimize

Clean topology, unwrapped UVs and budgets respected — assets arrive engine-ready, printable, or pipeline-clean.

  • Clean topology
  • UV / baking
  • Poly budget

Render & Deliver

Cinematic presentation renders plus exports in the formats your workflow needs — OBJ, FBX, GLTF, STL, USDZ, BLEND.

  • Studio renders
  • Turntable
  • All formats
